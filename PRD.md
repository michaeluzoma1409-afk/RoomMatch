# RoomMatch - PRD
Group 10 - Babcock University

## 1. Problem
At Babcock University, students don't get to choose their room or roommates. This causes conflict and affects our studies and peace of mind.Some students tend to be inconsiderate of others when they need to study.Also, some rooms can be a bit small or clustered for people to use.

## 2.Research
What we have discovered is that students are sometimes dissatisfied with the state of the room or the people they have to share a room with.we would like to make possible for them to choose their roommate, like friends or coursemates.

## 3. Solution
RoomMatch will match students with compatible roommates based on their habits, like sleep time, cleanliness, and study style.

## 4. Risks
The biggest risk is students lying because they want to be with their friends. We will reduce this by using verified habits and not allowing direct friend requests.

## 5. Users
- 100lvl Students
- Hall Admins
- Graduating students
- Returning students

## 6. Features
- Room allocation
- Roommates can be chosen by category

## 7. Success
- 60%

## 8. User Story
As a student, I want to be able to study peacefully in my room. I want to be able to talk with everyone fairly.
